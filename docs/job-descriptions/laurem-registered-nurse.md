## 1. Job purpose

The Registered Nurse provides safe, evidence-informed and person-centred nursing care within professional competence, the applicable care setting and the requirements of the NMC and Conags.

The postholder is responsible for recognising and responding to clinical needs, maintaining accurate records, escalating risk, working collaboratively and maintaining professional standards.

---

## 2. Key responsibilities

1. Assess, plan, deliver and evaluate nursing care within competence and applicable standards.
2. Recognise deterioration and escalate clinical, safeguarding and safety concerns promptly.
3. Administer medicines and undertake clinical interventions only where trained, competent and authorised.
4. Maintain accurate, timely and confidential clinical records.
5. Communicate effectively with service users, families, colleagues and multidisciplinary teams.
6. Follow infection prevention, health and safety, medicines governance and incident-reporting requirements.
7. Promote dignity, privacy, consent, choice and person-centred care.
8. Maintain required registration, professional standards, mandatory training and continuing development.
9. Participate in clinical supervision, induction, competency review and service improvement activities.
10. Use approved Conags workforce systems for rota, attendance, timesheets, leave and internal communication where instructed.

---

## 3. Person specification

### Essential
- Lawful right to work in the UK.
- Current NMC registration or an approved registration pathway appropriate to the role and lawful scope of practice.
- Completion of applicable identity, reference, safeguarding, disclosure/PVG and occupational checks.
- Ability to communicate effectively and exercise professional judgement.
- Commitment to safe practice, confidentiality, equality and safeguarding.

### Desirable
- Relevant post-registration experience.
- Additional clinical training relevant to the service.
- Experience of electronic clinical documentation and multidisciplinary working.

---

## 4. Professional accountability

The postholder must practise within the limits of their competence and professional authority and must comply with applicable NMC requirements and Conags procedures.

Where a task requires supervision, competency confirmation or local authorisation, the employee must follow the stated arrangements.

---

## 5. Attendance, performance and development

The employee must attend scheduled duties punctually, maintain accurate records of working time and participate in required supervision, appraisal, training and professional development.

Clinical and professional concerns must be raised promptly through the appropriate route.
