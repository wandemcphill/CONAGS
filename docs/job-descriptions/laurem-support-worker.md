## 1. Job purpose

The Support Worker assists service users with daily living, independence, wellbeing and participation in accordance with care plans, agreed support arrangements, training, competence and lawful instructions.

---

## 2. Key responsibilities

1. Provide respectful, person-centred support that promotes dignity, choice and independence.
2. Assist with daily living and community participation within the agreed support plan.
3. Observe and report changes in wellbeing, behaviour or circumstances.
4. Follow safeguarding, health and safety, infection prevention and confidentiality requirements.
5. Maintain accurate and timely records.
6. Escalate accidents, incidents, complaints and safeguarding or safety concerns promptly.
7. Work constructively with service users, families, colleagues and client teams.
8. Complete induction, mandatory training, supervision and competency requirements.
9. Use approved Conags workforce systems for rota, attendance, timesheets, leave and communication where instructed.
10. Maintain professional boundaries and respect confidentiality.

---

## 3. Person specification

### Essential
- Lawful right to work in the UK and completion of applicable pre-employment checks.
- Calm, respectful communication.
- Reliability and a person-centred approach.
- Willingness to undertake mandatory training and competency development.
- Commitment to safeguarding and confidentiality.

### Desirable
- Previous care, support or community-work experience.
- Relevant qualification or training.
- Experience using electronic records or workforce systems.

---

## 4. Working within competence

The employee must only undertake duties for which the employee is trained, competent, authorised and appropriately supervised.

---

## 5. Attendance, development and flexibility

The employee must attend scheduled duties punctually, follow applicable absence and timesheet procedures, participate in supervision and training, and remain open to reasonable changes in duties that are lawful and within the employee's competence and contractual framework.
