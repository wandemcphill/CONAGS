## 1. Job purpose

The Healthcare Assistant supports service users to live safely, comfortably and with dignity. The postholder provides person-centred care and practical support in accordance with care plans, agreed service arrangements, training, competence and lawful instructions.

The role requires a professional, compassionate and reliable approach and a clear commitment to safeguarding, confidentiality, equality and safe working practices.

The job description describes the principal responsibilities of the role. This document should be read together with the employment contract and Staff Handbook.

---

## 2. Key responsibilities

### Person-centred care
1. Provide respectful support with personal care and activities of daily living in accordance with the individual's care plan and preferences.
2. Support mobility, nutrition, hydration, comfort and emotional wellbeing within the scope of the role.
3. Promote dignity, privacy, independence, choice and participation wherever reasonably possible.
4. Respect cultural, communication, religious and personal preferences.

### Safety and safeguarding
5. Remain alert to signs of abuse, neglect, exploitation, deterioration or other risks.
6. Report safeguarding concerns, incidents, accidents, medication-related concerns and other serious risks promptly through the approved Conags process.
7. Follow infection prevention, health and safety, moving and assisting, fire safety and other applicable requirements.
8. Escalate changes in a service user's condition or circumstances to the appropriate senior person without unnecessary delay.

### Communication and records
9. Communicate clearly and professionally with service users, families, colleagues, managers and client teams.
10. Maintain accurate, timely and confidential records using the approved systems.
11. Share information only where authorised and necessary for legitimate care or operational purposes.
12. Participate appropriately in handovers, meetings, supervision and service reviews.

### Professional practice
13. Work within training, competence, supervision, the job description and authorised duties.
14. Complete required induction, mandatory training and competency assessments within the required timescales.
15. Use the Conags workforce platform for rota, attendance, timesheets, leave and internal communication where instructed.
16. Maintain appropriate professional boundaries and protect the reputation and confidentiality of Conags and the people it supports.

## 3. Working relationships

The postholder may work with:

- service users and their families or representatives;
- Conags managers and operational staff;
- other care and support workers;
- nurses or other qualified professionals where applicable; and
- client or placement teams where an assignment requires this.

The postholder is expected to communicate respectfully, cooperate with colleagues and raise concerns through the appropriate reporting route.

## 4. Person specification

### Essential
- Lawful right to work in the UK and completion of applicable pre-employment checks.
- Commitment to safe, person-centred care.
- Respectful and effective verbal and written communication.
- Reliability, professionalism and the ability to follow agreed procedures.
- Willingness to complete mandatory training and role-specific competency assessment.
- Ability to maintain confidentiality and appropriate professional boundaries.

### Desirable
- Previous experience in health, social care, support work or a related setting.
- Relevant care or support qualification.
- Experience working with care plans, safeguarding procedures and electronic records.
