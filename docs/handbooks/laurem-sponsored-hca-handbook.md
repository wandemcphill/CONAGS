# Conags Care
## Sponsored Healthcare Assistant Handbook

**Audience:** Healthcare Assistants employed by Conags Care Group Limited under a sponsored or other lawful UK work route.

**Document status:** Operational handbook template. It is not immigration or legal advice and must be kept aligned with the worker's actual immigration permission, employment contract, Conags policies and current UK/Scottish guidance.

---

## 1. Welcome to Conags

Welcome to Conags Care. This handbook explains what to expect as a Healthcare Assistant, how to work safely, how to use the workforce platform, where to get help and what to do if you have concerns about your work, immigration status, pay, accommodation or wellbeing.

Every worker is entitled to fair treatment, safe working conditions, respect and access to appropriate support. Sponsored workers have the same employment protections as other workers, subject to the conditions of their immigration permission.

## 2. Important immigration position

Conags must never promise overseas sponsorship for a new Healthcare Assistant role where the immigration rules do not permit it.

From 22 July 2025, the UK route for new care worker and senior care worker applications from overseas closed. During the transitional period, certain in-country applications can continue where the worker is already in the UK and meets the relevant conditions, including the required period of lawful work for the sponsoring provider. Conags' recruiter must verify the current immigration position before making any offer or assigning a Certificate of Sponsorship.

The worker must only undertake work permitted by their immigration status and must immediately tell Conags if their immigration permission changes, expires, is curtailed or restricts their employment.

## 3. Your role as a Healthcare Assistant

Healthcare Assistants support people with personal care, daily living, comfort, dignity, nutrition, hydration, mobility and other delegated care activities within their competence and care plan.

You must never undertake a task that you are not trained, competent or authorised to perform. When uncertain, stop and ask the senior person responsible for the service user.

## 4. Person-centred care

Conags expects care to be built around the individual rather than the task list. Respect preferences, routines, privacy, culture, beliefs, communication needs, choices and lawful care-plan instructions.

Never use discriminatory, humiliating, threatening or degrading language or behaviour.

## 5. Safeguarding

Protect children and protected adults from abuse, neglect and exploitation. Report concerns immediately through the Conags safeguarding route and emergency services where necessary.

Examples of concerns include unexplained injuries, financial abuse, sexual abuse, neglect, coercive control, institutional abuse, discriminatory abuse and significant unexplained changes in behaviour.

Do not investigate a safeguarding allegation yourself or promise secrecy to a person who discloses a concern.

## 6. Scotland: PVG and safer recruitment

Where your role is a regulated role requiring Protecting Vulnerable Groups (PVG) scheme membership in Scotland, Conags will manage the required process. PVG membership requirements must be satisfied before undertaking regulated work where legally required.

From 1 April 2026, new PVG membership operates on a five-year renewal cycle. Conags will track relevant renewal dates and will tell staff when action is required.

## 7. Registration and professional standards

Where the role requires professional registration or another statutory registration, you must maintain it throughout employment and tell Conags immediately about restrictions, conditions, suspension or removal.

Healthcare Assistants must follow applicable Scottish social services standards and the Conags competency framework.

## 8. Mandatory training

Before and during employment, Conags may require training in:

- safeguarding
- moving and assisting
- infection prevention and control
- medication awareness where relevant to the role
- health and safety
- basic life support or emergency response where applicable
- food hygiene where applicable
- fire safety
- information governance and confidentiality
- equality, diversity and inclusion
- mental capacity, consent and human rights principles
- professional boundaries
- incident reporting

Training completion is recorded in the Conags workforce platform.

## 9. Working time, rota and attendance

You must use the Conags workforce platform for your published rota, attendance, assignment confirmation and timesheet processes where instructed.

Arrive ready to work at the required time. If you are delayed, unable to attend or have an emergency, notify the designated Conags contact as soon as reasonably possible using the approved channel.

Never ask another worker to clock in or out for you.

## 10. Pay and payslips

Your pay is governed by your employment contract and approved rate. Conags will process lawful deductions and provide the relevant payroll information.

Raise any suspected payroll error promptly. Do not accept unexplained deductions without asking Conags for clarification.

## 11. Immigration and right-to-work compliance

Conags will complete the legally required right-to-work checks. Non-British and non-Irish workers may normally use the Home Office online right-to-work system and share code route where eligible.

Keep your UKVI account and digital immigration status details accurate. Notify Conags before any visa or permission deadline that could affect your ability to work.

## 12. Sponsorship responsibilities

Where Conags sponsors your role, you must:

- work in the sponsored role and location arrangements communicated by Conags, subject to lawful assignment rules
- keep contact and immigration details current
- attend required work and training
- cooperate with lawful compliance checks
- report significant changes affecting your immigration permission
- never allow another person to use your identity or work authorisation

Conags will meet its own sponsorship duties and will not pass prohibited sponsor fees or associated administrative costs to you.

## 13. Professional boundaries

Do not borrow money from service users, enter financial arrangements with them, accept inappropriate gifts, share personal financial details, form exploitative relationships or use your role for private gain.

Use only approved communication channels for work-related contact.

## 14. Confidentiality and digital conduct

Protect service-user information, staff information and Conags business information. Do not photograph, record, copy or post confidential information to social media or messaging groups.

Use personal devices for work only in accordance with Conags policy.

## 15. Incidents and accidents

Report accidents, near misses, medication errors, safeguarding concerns, complaints and other reportable incidents immediately through the approved process. Record facts accurately and promptly.

Never alter a clinical or care record to hide an error.

## 16. Absence and sickness

Notify Conags using the absence procedure. Do not attend work when doing so would create an unsafe infection or health risk.

Follow the sickness certification and return-to-work process when applicable.

## 17. Equality, dignity and inclusion

Conags values a workforce where nationality, race, religion, sex, disability, age, sexual orientation and other protected characteristics are respected. International staff must receive support to understand the local care context, and UK-based colleagues must support an inclusive workplace.

## 18. Travel and accommodation

Where Conags provides or arranges accommodation or travel support, the exact terms must be recorded separately. Employees should receive clear information about rent, deductions, tenancy terms and the period of any temporary accommodation.

Do not assume accommodation is free unless the written offer says so.

## 19. Complaints, grievance and whistleblowing

You can raise concerns about care quality, safeguarding, discrimination, harassment, pay, working time, immigration compliance, recruitment practices or other workplace matters without retaliation.

Use the Conags grievance and whistleblowing procedures. Serious immediate risks should be escalated through emergency or statutory channels as appropriate.

## 20. Support and wellbeing

Ask for help early. Conags may provide a manager, buddy, mentor or other support contact. International or sponsored workers should also be signposted to relevant trade unions, professional organisations, community networks and official government guidance.

## 21. Leaving employment

Notice and termination are governed by your employment contract and applicable law. Do not simply stop attending work, especially where immigration sponsorship is involved. Contact Conags so that any employment and immigration reporting obligations can be handled correctly.
